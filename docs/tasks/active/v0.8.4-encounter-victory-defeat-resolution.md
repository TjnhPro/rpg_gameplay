## 1. Objective — explicit assessment, not encounter termination

Implement **Encounter completion assessment** that can determine whether a *currently active* Encounter has enough explicit, canonical evidence to propose a **side-based victory/defeat or draw**. It must work for a small monster fight, multi-side PvP, headless dungeon-wave composition and turn-based game composition while preserving the canonical ownership of Entity, Combat, Skill, Effect and Actor AI.

| Feature | Scope | Required result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **EN21 — Completion Evidence** | Caller-provided current Entity Life facts, objective facts and continuation barrier | Closed, detached, immutable observation |
| **EN22 — Side Elimination Assessment** | Evaluate named contender sides using canonical Entity Life; never infer a defeat from an empty roster | Deterministic evaluation through L3 policy |
| **EN23 — Objective Completion** | Evaluate explicitly named objective facts and ordered L3 outcome mappings | No hardcoded boss, survival, capture-point or wave formula |
| **EN24 — Victory / Defeat / Draw** | Side-based winning/defeated IDs, or draw, with full identity and reason consistency | Immutable proposed outcome, not per-Actor reward |
| **EN25 — Resolution Candidate** | Return `resolvable`/`pending` assessment with the exact cause and selected rule | **No `active → resolved` transition** and no persisted outcome in this milestone |

A consumer must be able to evaluate the following without creating a game/World/Combat Engine:

```text
Encounter: forest-fight-001
Status: active
Contender sides: party, enemies
Participants: player-001 → party; monster-001 → enemies
Current canonical Life: player-001 alive; monster-001 dead
L4 continuation: clear (no pending wave/phase)
L3 rule: side elimination, contender sides [party, enemies]

Expected:
  status = resolvable
  outcome.kind = victory
  winnerSideIds = [party]
  defeatedSideIds = [enemies]
  EncounterState.status remains active
  Entity, Combat, Skill, Effect and Actor AI unchanged
```

By contrast, if the game announces another wave is pending, the *same Entity Life facts* must produce `pending / continuation_pending`, not an early victory. A missing Life snapshot, empty contender roster, unresolved objective, or a non-active Encounter must not become a fabricated win.

**EN25's “Resolution Candidate” means a detached candidate *decision/outcome*, not an `EncounterState` with `status:'resolved'`.** The authoritative `active → resolved` transition, outcome recording and later `closed`/`cancelled` operations belong to **v0.8.5 EN26–EN30**.

Do not reopen, reinterpret or silently change any approved selection:

| Decision | Approved A | Mandatory consequence in v0.8.4 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **ENC-D01** | Encounter represents one combat engagement, not an entire Zone/Dungeon | Dungeon/zone orchestrates multiple Encounters at L4; no dungeon manager inside L2 |

| **ENC-D03** | Generic side IDs | Outcomes name `sideId`s, never derive winners from `Entity.kind` |
| **ENC-D04** | Dynamic participation under L3 policy | Re-evaluate the *current* roster; no fixed roster/implicit forfeiture |
| **ENC-D05** | Explicit completion evaluation | No auto-end after one kill, one leave, an empty side or a completed wave |
| **ENC-D06** | Terminal closure; new encounter ID for rematch | No reopening or mutating terminal state in v0.8.4 |
| **ENC-D07** | Side-based outcomes | No actor-level reward, XP, loot, kill credit or separate winners by Player/Pet |

Task approval never authorizes an agent to modify RPG Core or duplicate an owner.

**Clarification of terms:**

- `defeatedSideIds` means **Encounter outcome classification**. It is not an instruction to set all those Entities' Life to `'dead'`.
- `draw` means there are no winners or defeated sides in the proposed outcome; it does not award points/rewards.
- “Explicit completion” includes an L4-provided *continuation barrier*: additional waves or unresolved phases can prevent final resolution even when current enemies are all dead.
- Side IDs express Encounter membership, **not** canonical Combat ally/enemy relations.

Canonical numerical formulas only. No new formula is requested. No use of Core Legacy gameplay entrypoints, no numeric health elimination heuristic, combat power, threat score, ranking or percentage threshold to determine winner.

### L2 — Encounter (this milestone)

Owns immutable completion observations, explicit rule policy validation, side-elimination assessment, objective rule interpretation, `pending`/`resolvable` decision, side outcome schema and correlation with Encounter ID/definition ID. Reads Entity presence and canonical Life *facts*, does not mutate Life.

### L2 — Entity / Combat / Skill / Effect / Actor AI

- **Entity:** identity/collection and `alive`/`dead` Life; canonical owner of HP/resources and defeat/revival transitions.
- **Combat:** admission, target, attack resolution/damage. An attack result alone is not an Encounter winner.
- **Skill:** admission/cost/cooldown/activation, execution outcomes. A Skill `accepted` is not completed execution.
- **Effect:** effect lifecycle, status/periodic restrictions, cleanup through existing canonical APIs.
- **Actor AI:** perception/targets/decision FSM/actions/timing/feedback; no Encounter win state.

Encounter production does **not** import those modules except Entity. L4 combines public APIs when required.

### L3 — Game Rules / Content

Owns definition-specific completion rules, ordered priorities, which sides contest a win, objective identity, mapping of objective status to winner/loser/draw, zero-survivor policy, wave expectations. No hardcoded dungeon, boss or PvP behavior in L2.

### L4 — Application / World

Owns authoritative current Encounter roster, current Life from Entity, proof/meaning of objective outcomes, explicit `continuation` fact, pending wave/phase provenance, permissions, cross-Encounter uniqueness, transactional commit, deduplication and eventual Event/Reward/World cleanup. Must refresh facts at commit; submitted observations are not trust tokens.

## 5. Completion contract design principles

1. **Completion assessment is read-only.** It returns a detached frozen decision; it does not update Encounter State, dispatch commands, publish events, grant rewards, close or cancel.
2. **L3 owns explicit policy.** L2 implements a generic **ordered rule evaluator**, not a hardcoded `allMonstersDead()` or `dungeonCleared()`.
3. **L4 owns all external truth.** Life from canonical Entity, objective facts from caller's World/objective owner, and wave/continuation status from caller. No L2 objective store or wave cursor.
4. **Incomplete evidence never means victory.** Missing participant Life fact, pending wave, an empty contender roster or unmet objective cannot turn into success by optimistic assumptions.
5. **Outcome is side-based.** Multiple winning sides may be specified for objective outcomes. L2 must not assign rewards or per-Actor defeat.
6. **Explicit deterministic ordering.** In a policy with multiple conditions, first satisfied rule wins. No RNG, hidden time, arbitrary expression evaluator, scoring or unordered Map iteration priority.
7. **Prepared/terminal states cannot be resolved by this evaluator.** Validate malformed inputs first, then return a normal pending reason. v0.8.5 owns the actual lifecycle status transition.
8. **No stale authorization.** A `resolvable` result is advisory; L4 cannot use it as a permission to bypass current membership/Life/World validation.

## 6. Proposed public API — deliberately small

The following types/functions are the target API **only if the accepted v0.8.3 HEAD does not already contain equivalent definitions**. Keep exact approved prior public signatures intact. Do not introduce hidden prerequisites or unapproved status fields.

### 6.1. Completion evidence

```ts
export interface EncounterParticipantLifeFact {
  readonly entityId: string;
  readonly life: EntityLifeState;
}

export type EncounterObjectiveStatus = 'pending' | 'succeeded' | 'failed';

export interface EncounterObjectiveFact {
  readonly objectiveId: string;
  readonly status: EncounterObjectiveStatus;
}

export type EncounterContinuation = 'pending' | 'clear';

export interface EncounterCompletionObservation {
  readonly encounterId: string;
  readonly participants: readonly EncounterParticipantLifeFact[];
  readonly objectives: readonly EncounterObjectiveFact[];
  readonly continuation: EncounterContinuation;
}
```

`continuation` is a **caller claim for this assessment**: `pending` = L4 says another wave/phase or external completion prerequisite remains; `clear` = L4 says no such continuation prevents final resolution. No `Date.now` or implicit next-wave check. L2 never verifies World truth by itself; L4 must enforce provenance/freshness. This fact exists even for headless/turn-based games; such games explicitly supply `clear` when appropriate.

### 6.2. Objective outcome mapping

```ts
export type EncounterVictoryOutcomeTemplate = {
  readonly kind: 'victory';
  readonly winnerSideIds: readonly string[];
  readonly defeatedSideIds: readonly string[];
};

export type EncounterDrawOutcomeTemplate = {
  readonly kind: 'draw';
  readonly winnerSideIds: readonly [];
  readonly defeatedSideIds: readonly [];
};

export type EncounterOutcomeTemplate =
  | EncounterVictoryOutcomeTemplate
  | EncounterDrawOutcomeTemplate;
```

Rules validate side IDs against **the assessed Encounter's declared `state.sides`** before any domain outcome. For `victory`: winner list is nonempty, winner/defeated lists are individually unique and disjoint, *each* ID names a declared side; defeated list may be empty only if L3 has explicitly defined a non-adversarial objective success. A contested objective should name its defeated sides. `draw` requires both arrays exactly empty; use no implicit winner/loser inference.

**Caution:** `kind:'victory'` represents a successful side-based outcome; `defeatedSideIds` is how defeats are represented. Do **not** add `kind:'defeat'` as an Encounter-global outcome without defining from whose perspective that would be measured.

### 6.3. Ordered L3 rules

```ts
export type EncounterCompletionRule =
  | {
      readonly ruleId: string;
      readonly kind: 'side_elimination';
      readonly contenderSideIds: readonly string[];
      readonly zeroSurvivors: 'pending' | 'draw';
    }
  | {
      readonly ruleId: string;
      readonly kind: 'objective';
      readonly objectiveId: string;
      readonly whenStatus: 'succeeded' | 'failed';
      readonly outcome: EncounterOutcomeTemplate;
    };

export interface EncounterCompletionPolicy {
  readonly definitionId: string;
  readonly rules: readonly EncounterCompletionRule[];
}
```

- `policy.definitionId` must equal `state.definitionId` **exactly**, preventing evaluation with another encounter template's L3 rules.
- `rules` array order is explicit priority. All rule IDs unique, exact opaque nonblank strings. `rules:[]` is valid and means **no condition can resolve**.
- `side_elimination.contenderSideIds`: **minimum two distinct side IDs**, preserve policy order, all must refer to declared sides. This restriction is for this *rule kind*, not activation: **one-sided Encounter activation remains valid** under v0.8.1. One-sided objective/survival encounters use `objective` rules.
- `zeroSurvivors:'draw'` makes a mutual defeat/zero-survivor draw eligible **only** when every contender side currently has at least one recorded participant and all those participant Life facts are known. `pending` instead keeps it unresolved.
- An `objective` rule checks exactly one current objective fact for the configured `objectiveId` and `whenStatus`, then projects the policy-declared side outcome. Objective rules are not arbitrary callbacks, scripts, generic expression trees or runtime content catalogs.
- Multiple objective rules with the same objective ID are allowed when they have distinct `ruleId`s (e.g. success vs failure); **declared policy order** breaks ties if multiple distinct facts/rules match. Observation duplicate objective IDs are forbidden.
- `winnerSideIds` and `defeatedSideIds` in objective outcome are explicit L3 choices, not inferred from `Entity.kind`, Combat relations, roster emptiness or objective ID string.
- Never mutate policy or reorder rules as a side effect of evaluation.

### 6.4. Proposed side-based result

```ts
export type EncounterResolutionOutcome =
  | {
      readonly kind: 'victory';
      readonly winnerSideIds: readonly string[];
      readonly defeatedSideIds: readonly string[];
    }
  | {
      readonly kind: 'draw';
      readonly winnerSideIds: readonly [];
      readonly defeatedSideIds: readonly [];
```

## 7. EN21 — Completion evidence and complete validation

### 7.1. Strict input shape

`createEncounterCompletionObservation` accepts **exactly** the four fields `encounterId`, `participants`, `objectives`, `continuation`. `participants` and `objectives` must be actual dense arrays. No `Map`, Set, sparse array, inherited numeric index, extra array property, getter/setter or symbol fields. Individual facts are closed own-data records; prototypes ordinary Object/null only.

Life must be captured using the **canonical accepted EntityLifeState shape**: exactly `entityId` and a canonical Life `'alive'|'dead'`. Require `fact.entityId === fact.life.entityId` exactly. No `isAlive`, resource-derived Life, generated ID or coercion. Objective facts are exactly `{objectiveId, status}` with allowed statuses `pending/succeeded/failed`. Continuation is exactly `pending/clear`.

Reject duplicate participant Life IDs and duplicate objective IDs. Preserve literal ID spelling, case and leading/trailing whitespace if nonblank; do not normalize. Return recursively frozen detached copies. Do not freeze the caller's inputs or call getter code.

### 7.2. Correlation to current Encounter

At evaluation time:

1. `validateEncounterState(state, entities)` must succeed; all current participant Entity IDs still exist in the canonical collection.
2. Capture and fully validate policy and observation, including **all rules** and **all** facts, even if the first rule would match or the Encounter is non-active.
3. Require `policy.definitionId === state.definitionId` and `observation.encounterId === state.encounterId`. Mismatches are **TypeError**, not normal pending results.
4. Any observation Life fact for an Entity **not currently in this Encounter roster** is a malformed foreign fact: `TypeError`. It may not silently become a Join, and facts from a previous roster must be rebuilt by L4.
5. Every policy contender/winner/defeated side ID must exist in `state.sides`; unknown references are `TypeError`, even when that rule is not selected. The `objectiveId` itself remains an opaque L3/L4 identity and need not exist in an Entity/Effect collection.
6. Missing Life facts for some roster participants are a **valid incomplete observation**, never assumed `'dead'`. Evaluation returns `pending / participant_fact_missing` for the first missing participant in accepted roster order (after the inactive/continuation barriers defined below).
7. Objective observations may be empty or omit configured objective IDs: missing objective means that specific rule **does not match**. A reported `pending` objective does not match `succeeded` or `failed`. Foreign objective IDs unrelated to current policy may be accepted as extra facts **only if structurally valid**, because L4 can submit multiple observed objectives; they cannot create an implicit victory.

### 7.3. No provenance overclaim

A well-formed `status:'dead'` fact is a caller observation, not cryptographic proof or a transferable receipt. L4 obtains actual current Entity Life and verifies revision/controller/world/encounter authority before commit. An objective status is a caller-owned fact, not a new L2 objective evaluator. `continuation:'clear'` is an explicit caller judgment, not permission to skip current wave/phase checking.

## 8. EN22 — Side elimination assessment

### 8.1. Exact source of truth

Side elimination considers only the configured `contenderSideIds` and the current Encounter roster, using **Entity Life snapshots supplied in the observation**. It must never read HP, damage, Skill accepted, kill events, World disappearance, Entity.kind, Side ID words, Actor AI target loss or Combat relations to derive a Life transition.

Side's living count = number of current *participants on that side* with reported canonical `life.status === 'alive'`. This count is a simple membership predicate/aggregation; it is **not** a new numerical combat engine or stat calculation.

### 8.2. Required conservative rule

A `side_elimination` rule is **not satisfied** when **any** named contender side has zero current participants. An empty side might mean an unspawned wave, a voluntary leave, an unfilled PvP slot, or a yet-to-be-joined opponent. L2 cannot interpret that absence as death/defeat.

With all contender sides populated and all roster Life facts complete:

| Alive contender side count | Rule outcome |
| --- | --- |
| 2 or more | Rule not satisfied; Encounter remains pending unless a later objective rule matches |
| Exactly 1 | `victory` for that surviving side, defeated side IDs = other named contender sides, in L3 contender order |
| 0 and `zeroSurvivors:'draw'` | `draw`, empty winners/defeated |
| 0 and `zeroSurvivors:'pending'` | Rule not satisfied; pending unless another rule matches |

No generic “all sides except the victor are defeated” deduction outside the explicitly named contender list. Neutral/support side IDs not listed in the rule are **ignored for that specific elimination condition**. They remain legitimate participants and retain their data; L3 must consciously choose its contender list.

### 8.3. Examples

```text
State sides: party, enemies, spectators
Rule contenders: [party, enemies]
Roster: P1 → party; M1 → enemies; N1 → spectators
Life: P1 alive, M1 dead, N1 alive
Continuation: clear

Proposed victory:
  winnerSideIds = [party]
  defeatedSideIds = [enemies]

Spectators not arbitrarily classified as losers.
```

```text
Roster: P1 → party; enemies side currently EMPTY
Continuation: clear

Outcome: pending (no_completion_condition_met)
Reason: empty opponent roster is NOT evidence of elimination.
```

### 8.4. Death does not mutate Encounter membership

Entity dead may remain an Encounter participant. Do not remove, despawn, resurrect or mark a participant “eliminated” in EncounterState. A later Life revival can change the next assessment. Once actual closure happens, rules for replay/reopening are decided in v0.8.5; this evaluator has no durable authority to remember earlier results.

## 9. EN23 — Objective-based completion

### 9.1. Explicit objective fact

Objective ID belongs to L3 content and L4 provenance, examples:

```text
capture-shrine
protect-crystal
survive-until-turn-10
boss-phase-completed
pvp-forfeit
```

These names have **no built-in semantics** in Encounter. L2 checks only whether current observation contains the exact `objectiveId` with status equal to the exact `whenStatus` declared by L3.

The same `objectiveId` may be configured by L3 for both success/failure in two separate ordered rules. Never infer a failure just because a success is absent. `pending` is not failure. A timeout/round limit is evaluated by L4's turn/runtime controller, then converted to explicit objective evidence; Encounter does not calculate or schedule time/turns.

### 9.2. Outcome mapping and priority

Example success:

```ts
{
  ruleId: 'protect-crystal-succeeded',
  kind: 'objective',
  objectiveId: 'protect-crystal',
  whenStatus: 'succeeded',
  outcome: {
    kind: 'victory',
    winnerSideIds: ['defenders'],
    defeatedSideIds: ['attackers'],
  },
}
```

Example failure from the same objective:

```ts
{
  ruleId: 'protect-crystal-failed',
  kind: 'objective',
  objectiveId: 'protect-crystal',
  whenStatus: 'failed',
  outcome: {
    kind: 'victory',
    winnerSideIds: ['attackers'],
    defeatedSideIds: ['defenders'],
  },
}
```

Example explicitly configured draw:

```ts
{
  ruleId: 'arena-draw',
  kind: 'objective',
  objectiveId: 'round-ended-draw',
  whenStatus: 'succeeded',
  outcome: {kind: 'draw', winnerSideIds: [], defeatedSideIds: []},
}
```

No auto-ranking, tie-breaking by damage, hidden score, kill credit, party contribution, or game-specific quest state.

### 9.3. Wave/dungeon continuity

`continuation:'pending'` is a **global assessment barrier**: no rule may return `resolvable` while L4 knows another wave/phase/prerequisite remains. Example:

```text
Wave 1 all monsters dead, wave 2 not yet spawned
L4 continuation = pending
=> pending / continuation_pending
```

When wave 2 has spawned and finished, **L4 supplies a fresh observation and current roster**. It then explicitly sets `continuation:'clear'` only if the encounter genuinely has no further continuation; L2 still applies current policy rules. No WaveManager, wave cursor, spawn schedule or dungeon transaction is introduced.

An L3 objective rule may model a final dungeon objective, but one Encounter is still one engagement (ENC-D01). L4 sequences distinct Encounter instances if the dungeon requires multiple encounters.

## 10. EN24 — Winner / defeated sides / draw

### 10.1. Exactly one proposed outcome

When one ordered rule is satisfied and the global barriers are clear, return **one** outcome only. For `victory`:

- `winnerSideIds` follows the explicit L3 policy side order; do not sort.
- `defeatedSideIds` follows L3 contender order for elimination or explicit L3 template order for objectives.
- No duplicates and no overlap between these arrays.
- Every side ID must be declared in the Encounter State.
- Side victory/defeat classification is not a modification of the Entity's status, Combat session, Actor AI target, World location or Reward ledger.

For `draw`, winners and defeated are exactly empty arrays. A zero-survivor draw only occurs if L3 explicitly selected that policy and all contender rosters are populated with known Life facts, with continuation clear.

### 10.2. Multiple winning sides

Objective rules may explicitly list multiple winner IDs, including in 3+-side encounters, e.g. `winnerSideIds:['alpha','beta']` and `defeatedSideIds:['gamma']`. This does not infer an alliance from Combat relation or imply any personal reward. If L3 needs a game-specific alliance algorithm, it must first provide its externally computed objective and configured result.

### 10.3. No optimistic result or persistence

`resolvable` means only "this pure evaluator would propose this side outcome from these supplied facts". It is **not** a claim that the fight actually ended. `state.status` remains active and no persistent outcome companion is created here. L4 must guard against two concurrently evaluated candidates, stale World objectives, simultaneous kills and stale roster membership before future authoritative resolution.

## 11. EN25 — Evaluation order, pending outcomes, immutable proposal

### 11.1. Structural checks first, regardless of no-op

Before evaluating state status or an easy first rule:

1. Validate/detach complete EncounterState against canonical EntityCollection through accepted `validateEncounterState`.
2. Validate/detach complete policy, including every rule, subrecord and side ID list.
3. Validate/detach complete observation including all Life/objective facts and arrays.
4. Validate Encounter and definition identity correlation.
5. Validate every policy side reference resolves against `state.sides` and no observation participant fact is foreign.
6. Only then perform normal domain assessment. Invalid input throws `TypeError`, even when Encounter is `closed` or a wave is pending. Never call a getter while testing an invalid record.

### 11.2. Global normal-outcome precedence

After all structural validation:

| First failing condition | Assessment | Details |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `state.status !== 'active'` | `pending / encounter_not_active` | `ruleId:null`, `outcome:null`, `participantEntityId:null` |
| `observation.continuation === 'pending'` | `pending / continuation_pending` | No objective/elimination rule may resolve |
| First `state.participants` member has no matching Life fact | `pending / participant_fact_missing` | `participantEntityId` = first missing canonical roster ID |
| First policy rule satisfied | `resolvable / rule_matched` | `ruleId` exact matching rule ID; immutable `outcome` |
| No policy rule satisfied | `pending / no_completion_condition_met` | No fabricated draw, no auto-close |

The `participant_fact_missing` check occurs in **canonical participant array order** and is independent of the observation fact order. A foreign Life fact is TypeError even when other facts are missing.

### 11.3. Rule traversal

Iterate policy.rules in order. A nonmatching rule is skipped; evaluate the next. The first matching rule wins. If side elimination cannot conclude because 2+ contenders are alive, zero contender roster exists, or zero live sides with policy `pending`, **continue to later rules**, including objectives. A satisfied objective does not bypass the global `continuation:'pending'` or Life fact completeness barrier.

The policy and observation may be byte-for-byte equivalent under different object allocation/observation array order; results must be semantically identical. Do not iterate an unordered fact Map to choose outcomes. Never allow a rule with a missing objective status to match “failed” implicitly.

### 11.4. All results are detached/frozen

New `EncounterCompletionAssessment`, nested outcome and side-ID arrays must be detached, deeply frozen and independent from policy/observation/EncounterState. The caller's originals remain unchanged and unfrozen. There is no proposed mutated `EncounterState` and no `lastResolution`, `round`, `phase`, `victory`, `winner` or `resolutionAt` field added to prior state.

### 11.5. Caller authority

L4 may **display** a provisional assessment but cannot treat it as a final award/close receipt. In v0.8.5, a lifecycle transition must freshly validate current facts and accepted Encounter baseline instead of blindly trusting a passed result.

## 16. Immutability, validation and deterministic replay

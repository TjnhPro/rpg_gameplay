## 6. EN31 — Canonical Participant Life Reassessment

### 6.1. Life facts are sampled anew for each decision

For each decision cycle, test-local L4 must:

1. Read **accepted** Encounter state and its **current** roster in order.
2. Read current canonical Entity Life for **every current roster member** (independent Entity Life owner), build exact `EncounterParticipantLifeFact` records from that snapshot.
3. Supply `continuation` and objective facts explicitly to the **existing** `createEncounterCompletionObservation` / EN25 evaluator.
4. Let accepted EN25 distinguish `pending`/`resolvable`; only call `resolveEncounter` against **fresh** policy/facts, never a cached assessment or fabricated outcome.
5. Compare input revision/fact freshness against the authoritative current store **before** terminal commit. Dead participant IDs remain in roster until explicit approved leave; no automatic removal.

Do not let an earlier dead Life snapshot remain authoritative after Entity revival. Do not synthesize Life by reading Encounter membership or calculating HP directly. Do not infer a missing Life fact as `dead`. Do not read Life through an unrelated Entity ID or a stale side membership.

### 6.2. Actual life transition composition (test-only)

Use real public Entity/Combat methods to prove:

```text
Player alive HP100; Monster alive HP20
Real Combat hit applies20 → canonical Entity HP0 and Life dead
Encounter membership unchanged: both participants still present
L4 resamples two Entity Life facts → monster dead
EN25 side-elimination may propose party victory ONLY if continuation clear
and all configured rule/participant evidence requirements are satisfied.
```

Also prove Combat dodge or zero applied damage **does not fabricate death**, resource value alone cannot bypass canonical `EntityLifeState`, and a revived participant reports `alive` on the next explicit observation. Existing action scope checks remain independent of completion admission.

### 6.3. Structural correctness and deterministic precedence

- Missing canonical Entity ref: `validateEncounterState` TypeError, not `participant_fact_missing`.
- Current roster member lacking Life fact: accepted EN25 `pending / participant_fact_missing` (unless higher accepted global reason `encounter_not_active` or `continuation_pending` applies).
- Duplicate, foreign or Life ID-mismatched fact: TypeError on complete validation; no early `pending` loophole.
- Missing participant reason must name the **first unmatched current roster member in canonical order**; input observation order cannot choose the failure.
- No `pending` or `resolvable` result changes Entity Life, Encounter state, side assignments, Skill/Effect/AI state, time or RNG.

## 7. EN32 — Reinforcement / Wave Continuity (without a Wave Engine)

### 7.1. Accepted continuation barrier is authoritative only when revalidated by L4

- `continuation:'pending'` **blocks all configured normal completion** under EN25, including an apparently satisfied elimination or objective rule; EN26 must reject with the **same** pending reason.
- `continuation:'clear'` removes the barrier but still requires complete current Life facts and one satisfied L3 completion rule; it does not imply a win, close, despawn, give loot or spawn the next wave.
- L3 declares whether the engagement has a subsequent wave/phase or objective; L4 supplies the fact **for the current evaluation**. L2 is not permitted to determine whether a dungeon wave is queued by looking at Monster IDs, count, spawn points or timers.
- End of wave1 can leave active roster with only dead enemies. **Do not** clear that roster automatically; L4 may join new enemies via the accepted membership API.

### 7.2. Explicit reinforcement sequence

```text
Encounter.active, side enemies has wolf-001 dead
L4 says continuation pending → EN25 pending, no disposition
L4 spawns wolf-002 through World/Entity (L4 only; outside Encounter implementation)
L4 calls existing EN11 join + EN15 apply membership with current Life/readiness
L4 CAS-commits new roster; old wolf-001 remains unless explicit leave
L4 resamples Life facts for every current member, including wolf-001 AND wolf-002
L4 says pending until configured remaining waves/objectives are no longer queued
After final wolf-002 defeat, L4 supplies `clear` and EN25 runs against full roster
Only EN26 + authoritative L4 commit can propose and accept final resolution
```

EN15 joining a **dead** reinforcement must follow the actual L3 `allowedJoinLifeStatuses`, not a new default. An absent/duplicate/misassigned Entity must be rejected by canonical membership checks. If joining an Entity mid-combat violates a turn-based L3 policy, leave operation `rejected/operation_not_allowed`; do not sneak it past the existing rule.

### 7.3. No implicit wave cursor

No `advanceEncounterWave()`, `spawnWave()`, `WaveState`, `waveCount`, `nextSpawnAt`, `waveSeed`, `WaveClock`, `DungeonSession`, `encounterTurnIndex` or background producer at L2. A test-local L4 fixture may hold `worldWaveCursor` or `pendingWaveCount` to **exercise an explicit caller policy**, but that counter belongs to the fixture and must not be a public Gameplay Encounter export. Do not perform World spawn via a fake `addEntity` hidden in `Encounter`.

### 7.4. Empty roster safety

- An `active` Encounter whose enemies leave is not automatically resolved, defeated, cancelled or closed.
- An Encounter with **zero** participants is structurally possible (from explicit leave); no optimistic victory. Accepted EN25 side-elimination rules require actual contender evidence; objective rules may still resolve when correctly configured and all accepted prerequisites hold.
- When L4 had previously announced `clear` but discovers pending reinforcements before committing resolution, it rejects **the stale candidate** rather than pretending `clear` is irrevocable.

## 8. EN33 — Leave / Rejoin / Revive Coordination

### 8.1. Three distinct owners / operations

| Operation | Canonical owner | Allowed semantics |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Leave participant | Encounter EN11–EN15 | Removes membership reference only; does not kill/despawn/reward. |
| Revive Entity | Entity `reviveEntity` | Changes canonical Life under explicit resource/HP prerequisites; no membership or AI controller mutation. |
| Rejoin participant | Encounter EN11–EN15 | New policy/readiness-gated join; does not mean automatic respawn or implicit `activateEncounter`. |

Life and membership are **orthogonal**. A dead Entity may remain a participant; a revived Entity may remain outside the Encounter; a participant leaving an Encounter may stay alive in World. Never rewrite historical EN11–EN15 semantics to force rejoin or normalize the `sideId` silently.

### 8.2. Required explicit flows

**Flow A — leave then rejoin:**

```text
active: player-001→party, wolf-001→enemies
EN12 leave player-001 admitted (if configured) → proposed roster [wolf-001]
L4 commits current revision; Entity player-001 stays alive, unchanged
L4 refreshes current Life/worldReady; EN11 join player-001→party admitted if L3 allows
L4 commits new roster [wolf-001, player-001] (append ordering is canonical)
No automatic AI enable, Combat replay, reward, side restoration from a cache
```

**Flow B — dead member revived while still in Encounter:**

```text
active: player-001→party, wolf-001→enemies; player Life dead, still a member
L4 uses canonical Entity resource recovery + reviveEntity explicitly
L4 obtains player Life alive; membership unchanged
EN25 reassesses from fresh facts; any older defeat candidate must be invalidated
No Encounter state change or automatic reactivation by revival
```

**Flow C — dead participant left, revived and explicitly rejoined:**

```text
active → leave dead participant (if membership policy allows)
L4 revives Entity outside Encounter; Encounter still lacks participant
L4 obtains current Life + World readiness; join policy may admit/reject
If admitted, new candidate is committed by L4 before actions/evaluation resume
```

**Flow D — terminal state:** `resolved`, `closed`, `cancelled` cannot accept join/leave/change-side or revive the same Encounter. Revival of an external Entity **does not reopen** an already-finalized fight; a rematch uses a new Encounter ID per ENC-D06.

### 8.3. Policy-driven rejects and stable ordering

Use the exact accepted EN15 failure reasons and precedence; do not invent a `rejoin` special case in L2. For example:

- Join while `active` disallowed → `operation_not_allowed` after complete structural validation.
- Rejoin existing participant → `already_participating`; not a new slot.
- Join with Life dead when `allowedJoinLifeStatuses:['alive']` → `join_life_not_allowed`.
- World not ready when policy requires ready → `join_world_not_ready`.
- Join into undeclared side → `side_not_found`.
- Rejoin after `closed` → `encounter_not_open` (normal domain rejection, full schema checks first).

All candidate arrays remain immutable and detached. Append/remainder order is the accepted EN15 order, **not** original roster order reconstructed from an invisible history.

## 9. EN34 — Stale Transition Protection (test-local L4 authority)

### 9.1. What L2 can and cannot prove

Accepted L2 functions validate **structural and supplied snapshots**. They do not see network, actual World revision, authoritative Entity Life, pending wave source, transaction database or whether another caller already committed a candidate. A forged but well-formed `EncounterCompletionObservation` is **not an authenticated proof**. A pure `resolveEncounter` may yield the same candidate twice from the same inputs; **L4 must not accept it twice**.

### 9.2. Required L4 fixture authority (tests only)

Model in `tests/consumers/encounter-continuity-recovery.mjs`, **not** production:

```ts
// Test-fixture pseudocode; not a public new API or production schema.
interface LocalAuthoritativeEncounter {
  readonly revision: number;                // fixture-local only
  readonly state: EncounterState;             // accepted state
  readonly disposition: EncounterDispositionRecord|null;
  readonly worldFactsVersion: number;        // fixture-local only
  readonly entityLifeFactsVersion: number;   // fixture-local only
  readonly continuationVersion: number;     // fixture-local only
}
```

The fixture may use a single logical generation/epoch covering all input sources **if it demonstrably changes whenever a relevant Life/World/continuation/objective/roster fact changes**. A revision increment that tracks only `EncounterState` is **insufficient** for a stale-Life/world check. Capture exactly what the candidate read, revalidate immediately before commitment and compare to authoritative values. No timer, DB, external network or global singleton.

**Required commit protocol:**

1. Read `acceptedState`, `disposition`, revision and current external Life/objective/wave/controller facts.
2. Stage a candidate using existing L2 functions; preserve original baseline, evidence and rule bindings; do not update authoritative data during evaluation.
3. Before commit, re-read Encounter baseline, Entity Life source, World continuation/objective facts and relevant Actor AI/manual controller if action dispatch is involved.
4. If **any relevant fact or authoritative revision changed**, **reject** the old candidate with an L4-local `stale_*` diagnostic. Do not call this a new L2 rejection enum or modify the Encounter public API.
5. If still current, atomically accept full EN15 candidate or EN26 `(state, disposition)` **together**; if terminal, no second resolution/cancellation may win.
6. `closeEncounter` may operate only after an **accepted** resolved pair; cleanup handoff only after terminal state commit. No double payout/despawn/tick/AI dispatch caused by stale retries.

A JSON string equality check without validated snapshot and version/provenance is **not sufficient** as the sole concurrency authority; use explicit generation/baseline and compare current facts. In single-process tests, an in-memory authoritative fixture with controlled interleavings demonstrates the requirement; this does **not** prove distributed exactly-once or database transaction durability.

### 9.3. Mandatory race matrix

| Competing change after staging | Stale candidate | Required L4 outcome |
| --- | --- | --- |
| EN15 leave actor | Old action scope / completion candidate | Reject old source/roster baseline. |
| EN15 join reinforcement | Earlier `resolvable` / EN26 resolution | Reject; re-evaluate with new roster and Life evidence. |
| EN15 side transfer | Old winner-side/elimination mapping | Reject; use current side bindings. |
| Entity Life dead → alive (revive) | Earlier defeat/victory candidate | Reject; fresh Life may change winner. |
| Entity Life alive → dead | Earlier continuation/action candidate | Reject; recheck canonical admission. |
| World continuation clear → pending | Earlier `resolvable` candidate | Reject, no terminal disposition. |
| Objective pending → succeeded/failed | Old objective assessment | Reject; rerun ordered policy. |
| Controller AI → manual | Earlier AI dispatch intent | Reject at L4; no extra payment/damage. |
| Simultaneous resolve vs cancel | Two status/disposition proposals | Exactly one pair can be committed. |
| Simultaneous close vs repeat close | Old close proposal | At most one accepted terminal close. |
| Cleanup before terminal commit | Pure handoff may structurally be constructible | L4 must not execute cleanup; no World effects. |
| Repeated cleanup handoff after commit | Equivalent L2 snapshot | No duplicate L4 World side effect via fixture-local ledger. |

### 9.4. No false safety claims

- Do **not** add `version` into five-field EncounterState or a public `EncounterCommitReceipt` simply to pass CAS tests.
- Do **not** rely exclusively on EN25 `continuation:'pending'` to prove a wave fact was fresh.
- Do **not** rely on object reference identity, `Object.isFrozen`, or the fact a candidate was created by an API as proof it came from the accepted World baseline.
- No retry/fallback policy or refund is invented after stale rejection. L4 explicitly decides when to resample/restart.
- L2 must not publish “exactly once” or “transactional reward/cleanup” guarantees. Fixture evidence is scoped to controlled local interleavings.

## 10. EN35 — Deterministic Continuity & Recovery

### 10.1. Two independent sessions, not the same object replayed

Run at least **two independently created** fixture worlds/Entity collections/Encounter states, with equivalent explicit input sequences and provider seeds (if the actual Combat API consumes RNG). Each session must have its own actor state, observation arrays, order-preserving side roster, decision/world/revision/cleanup ledgers. ### 10.2. Literal semantic oracle

Test must assert **every meaningful stage** against a separately authored literal expected trace, not compute the expected values using the same function under test. Example:

```text
T0: Encounter EC-R1 prepared; player→party, wolf1→enemies
T1: explicit activate → active, revision1
T2: actual hit20 from HP20 → wolf1 HP0/Life dead

T8: L4 commits resolved + {kind:resolution,winner:[party],defeated:[enemies]}

T10: cleanup handoff only references [player,wolf1,wolf2], not World deletion
```

Roster ordering, side membership and `EncounterState` five keys must remain exact. Distinguish `assessment` from `resolve` candidate and `resolve` candidate from **L4 accepted** state+record. Completion remains pending until final clear + complete Life facts. ### 10.3. Time, RNG and effects

- For decisions not requiring a Clock/RNG, fail tests if production/fixture calls `Date.now`, `new Date()` for hidden decision time, `Math.random`, `setTimeout`, or an unapproved scheduler.
- For actual Combat samples, pass an explicit deterministic Foundation RNG through existing public owner; record exact draw count and literal HP/Life outcomes.
- If test-local World uses a tick/turn index, it is explicit caller input and must not be added to the Encounter public snapshot.
- Two executions with equivalent inputs/order match byte-for-byte **canonical serialized semantic traces**; a different explicit input must predictably alter output. Avoid hashes as sole oracle; hashes are supplementary evidence to stage-level assertions.
- All inputs/outcomes are detached and appropriately frozen; original caller policy and fact collections are not mutated or frozen as side effects.

## 12. Required independent L4 integration profiles

Test fixtures are **external application examples**, not production Encounter code. All imports must be from the installed public `@rpg/gameplay` package root, plus approved `@rpg/core/kernel` only where required by existing consumers. ### Profile A — Open-world PvE / reinforcement

1. L3 sets side IDs `party`, `enemies`, active join permitted, explicit `continuation` pending until the final expected reinforcement is known.
2. L4 creates canonical Player and two Monster Entity identities; the initial Encounter roster contains Player + Monster1 only.
3. L4 starts the prepared Encounter using **actual** EN06–EN10 public activation, current Entity Life and readiness; only the returned candidate is committed.
4. A real Combat pipeline defeats Monster1 through Combat + Entity Life; EN25 cannot finalize with pending continuation.
5. L4 spawns/creates Monster2 outside Encounter; uses **actual** membership policy/readiness to join; commits one candidate after a current-baseline check.

### Profile B — Dungeon boss or objective with multiple waves

- Use one Encounter only where content says one continuing engagement; **do not** turn Encounter into a full Dungeon manager.
- Pending continuation blocks side elimination **and** an otherwise satisfied objective rule, according to EN25 global precedence.
- A changed objective/continuation state invalidates any earlier candidate at L4, even if `EncounterState` fields have not changed.
- Final objective resolution uses L3 rule-provided side outcomes, not inferred kills or Actor types. Multi-side configured outcomes retain the original side arrays and rule ordering.

### Profile C — Turn-based fixed roster

- L3 membership policy disallows active joins/leave/change-side as applicable. No per-tick scheduler is imported; the caller supplies turn events.

- An attempted active reinforcement receives the **existing** `operation_not_allowed`, not a special turn-based status.

### Profile D — AI/manual + pet/summon continuity

- Player/Monster/NPC/Pet/Summon/Companion use the same generic Entity/Encounter references; no new Entity.kind tests in Encounter production.
- One actor can be AI-controlled while another is manual; Encounter membership grants no Actor AI execution authority.
- A stale AI action proposed before participant leave/side change/death must be rejected by **current** Encounter Action Scope and canonical admission at L4 dispatch.
- Pet self-skill obeys canonical Skill/Actor AI handling; any resource payment or cooldown belongs to Skill and **must not be replayed** by a stale Encounter retry.

### Profile E — Terminal cancellation / cleanup

- An L3-permitted cancellation while `active` produces `cancelled + cancellation record`, not synthetic draw.
- L4 chooses between concurrent resolution and cancellation using one authoritative revision / current fact observation; only **one pair** may be accepted.
- Closing a resolved Encounter does not delete it. Cleanup is a post-commit L4 operation whose underlying World/Effect actions are not performed by the L2 handoff.
- A repeated handoff may be identical; controlled fixture ledger prevents second downstream World side effect. Never claim durable exactly-once.

## 13. Explicit invariant / failure semantics

| Boundary | Required invariant | Failure semantics |
| --- | --- | --- |
| State | Exactly **five** fields, exact side/member IDs, same accepted enum | Malformed closed record → TypeError; well-formed not-open membership request → existing rejection envelope. |
| Entity | One canonical Life owner; no inferred dead/alive from HP in Encounter | Wrong Life ID / invalid status → existing TypeError. No hidden `reviveEntity`. |
| Observations | One exact Life fact per observed Entity, no foreign or duplicate facts | Invalid facts → TypeError; absent current member fact → existing EN25 pending reason after accepted precedence. |
| Continuation | Only explicit `pending` / `clear` from caller | `pending` blocks completion; invalid discriminant → TypeError, not fallback to `clear`. |
| Membership | Use current accepted roster, existing EN15 admission/transition | Rejected candidate `state:null`; no partial mutation or auto World spawn. |
| Policy | L3 configures join/waves/objectives; strict validation | Never infer defaults, reverse rule priority or use Entity.kind as side. |
| Completion | Re-evaluate current inputs with EN25 / EN26 | `resolvable` not committed outcome; pending never creates disposition. |
| Terminal | `resolved→closed`, explicit cancel only from allowed statuses | `closed/cancelled` cannot reopen; no synthetic outcome/reward. |
| Freshness | L4 owns revision and external-fact authority | Stale *L4* commit rejects, zero authoritative writes; do not add L2 revision field. |
| Cleanup | EN30 pure IDs-only handoff after terminal acceptance | Handoff not World execution; L4 can dedupe/retry without resetting status. |
| Reproducibility | Same explicit inputs/initial authority → same semantic output | Hidden RNG/clock/timer/global shared cache forbidden; no test-only fake oracle computed by API itself. |

### 13.1. Distinguish four result levels

```text
L2 membership/completion evaluation      ≠ L2 immutable transition candidate
L2 immutable transition candidate        ≠ L4 accepted authoritative commit
L4 terminal state+disposition commit     ≠ World cleanup / reward receipt
L4 World cleanup/reward receipt          ≠ L2 exactly-once/durable guarantee
```

### 13.2. Validate even on no-op/terminal cases

Do **not** short-circuit early on `state.status`, `continuation:'pending'`, nonexistent member, already-terminal status or denied policy **before** accepted full structural/correlation validation. Preserve v0.8.1/2/4/5 exact validation order: malformed nested Life, unknown side in policy, foreign fact or accessor must not be accidentally ignored because a case is “not applicable.” Verify accessor getters are **not invoked**.

### 13.3. Fail-closed L4 feedback and recovery

- Stale L4 action/commit returns **fixture-local** rejection diagnostic; no implicit retry or fallback; L4 initiates a **new** explicit evaluation with re-sampled truth.
- No preemptive cost refund, cooldown rollback, effect clear, AI reset, death revival, reward publication or cleanup after a failure. Canonical owners decide their own transitions.
- Snapshot comparison and authorization checks precede any irreversible operation in fixture. Inject a fault after staging and assert nothing is paid/damaged/closed/cleaned up due to that stale stage.
- A deferred World/cleanup operation that failed after an accepted terminal commit is retried by L4 using its own receipt/dedupe; the Encounter stays terminal.

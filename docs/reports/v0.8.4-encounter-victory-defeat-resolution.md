## 5. EN21 completion evidence

objectives, continuation. Participant facts bind entityId to canonical nested
EntityLifeState identity/status alive|dead; objectives preserve explicit

## 6. EN22 named side elimination

createEncounterCompletionPolicy binds definitionId and ordered rules. Elimination
requires at least two unique declared contenderSideIds and explicit zeroSurvivors
pending|draw. Any empty contender roster prevents that rule from resolving. Among
nonempty contender rosters, one side with an alive member wins; defeated sides
retain contender order. Multiple living sides keep evaluating later rules; zero
survivors draw only by explicit policy. Living noncontenders do not affect outcome.
Leaving/changing sides does not fabricate a defeat; fresh Life handles revival.

## 7. EN23 objective evaluation and continuation

Objective rule matches exact objectiveId/whenStatus succeeded|failed, then uses
explicit L3 side outcome template. Missing/pending objectives do not match. First
matching policy rule wins, independent of observation fact order. A global
continuation_pending barrier blocks both objective and elimination resolution,
including all-dead wave1. New L4 wave2/current Life facts are evaluated fresh.
Encounter spawns nothing, never detects disconnect or invents a survival formula.

## 8. EN24 immutable side outcome

Victory has nonempty unique winners and unique disjoint defeated IDs; objective
victory may have no defeated sides. All references must be declared in state.
Draw has both arrays empty. Templates preserve exact L3 order, including coalition
winners beta/alpha and defeated delta/gamma. No global defeat discriminator, actor
reward, Life mutation, score, loot or payout. Outcome is a detached proposal only.

## 9. EN25 assessment and precedence

evaluateEncounterCompletion(state, entities, policy, observation) fully validates
state/collection, all rules/facts and cross references before any domain return.
Precedence: encounter_not_active → continuation_pending → participant_fact_missing
(first current roster member) → first matched rule → no_completion_condition_met.
Pending has ruleId:null/outcome:null and nullable participantEntityId. Resolvable
has reason:rule_matched, exact ruleId, participantEntityId:null and frozen outcome.
Prepared/resolved/closed/cancelled never resolve. No active→resolved transition,
candidate state, stored outcome, receipt, session, engine or cache is introduced.

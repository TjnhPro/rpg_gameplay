## D. Limitations

No Skill Engine/Definition runtime, effect/damage/cooldown calculation, automatic
rewards/events, generic talent graph, respec/refund or persistent authority.
Caller must thread latest rank/point snapshots and jointly persist combined results.
Stale snapshots can branch; no database/global exactly-once guarantee. Structural
PG09 receipts cannot authenticate provenance. Point pool types are structural;
caller must keep Attribute/Skill domains separate. No cross-pool transaction.

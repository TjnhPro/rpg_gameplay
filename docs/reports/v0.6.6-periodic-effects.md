## 11. Capture and cursor state

One entry per canonical instance, explicit none or periodic(kind, intervalMs, resourceId, nextTickAtMs). State contains Entity/time/entries only; no embedded instance/lifetime authority. Empty-only initialization. New membership requires explicit capture; replacements on refresh/non-addition throw TypeError, consistent with other companions. All snapshots detached/frozen.

## 12. First tick

`startedAtMs + intervalMs`; never immediate. Literal start1000/interval1000 → first2000;1999 idle. Finite positive fractional intervals supported. Addition/completion fails if timestamp addition overflows or cannot progress.

## 13. Timed/persistent

Due timestamp must be <=clock and <=expiry inclusive. Duration shorter than interval produces no tick for both kinds. Persistent effects have no expiry ceiling and survive advancement. No tick after expiry.

## 14. Catch-up/order

One earliest intent, ordered timestamp then lexical instance ID. Completion adds one interval; caller repeats until idle. A jump1000→6000 yields2000/3000/4000/5000/6000, no clamping to observation time. Independent mixed intervals and same-time ties have literal expected traces.

## 23. Completion/stale rejection

Exact earliest due intent must match all six fields. Too early, duplicate, mismatched or out-of-order intent rejects stale_tick/state:null/events:[]; dead target and regressed time have explicit rejection. Completion advances one cursor only and emits immutable tick-completed metadata at scheduled timestamp. It is a candidate, not proof of Entity execution, persistence or concurrency freshness. L4 must prevent reuse of old structural state and verify actual successful execution.

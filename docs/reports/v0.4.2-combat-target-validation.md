caller-owned facts; structural receipts prove neither provenance nor freshness.

persistent ticket or concurrent authority. Caller must sequence stages and supply
current collection/Life/policy. accepted means supplied targeting policy matched,
not that an attack executed or remains authorized at a later commit.

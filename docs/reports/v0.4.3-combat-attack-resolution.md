## B. Implementation

CB16: resolveCombatAttack requires own-data admitted/none and accepted/none
receipts; captures canonical contexts and compares full action identity, target
ID, relation/self consistency and canonical Life status. It does not rerun
admission, target lookup or policies. Caller explicitly invokes resolution for
opaque action types including basic_attack; no Skill dependency/catalog.

CombatAttackInput composes public Kernel input/scaling/bonus contracts, replacing
B0 callbacks with ordered snapshots and explicit flat data, omitting Kernel roll
and mitigation damage (derived from B1). Every stage/numerical input is validated
and detached before any draw: closed own-data records, dense own-data arrays,
finite numbers, safe positive rank, nonblank IDs, unique scaling sources, bounds,
boolean flags and complete enabled mitigation config. Explicit empty scaling and
bonuses are required; no numerical defaults or coercion.

CB17/CB18: lazy roll closures call Foundation nextExecutionRandom. Hit precedes
Critical. Dodge returns only context/outcome/hit/finalDamage0. Disabled/ineligible
stages draw0; dodge chance0 draws0; eligible enabled Critical draws1 even at chance0.
Invalid/missing/throwing RNG fails with no retry/default/rollback.

CB19: actual Kernel calls own all arithmetic. Critical.bonus goes to B1 once;
multiplier is diagnostic only. Already-effective bonuses are not re-resolved.
Enabled mitigation requires positive finite baseK, nonnegative finite levelPenalty,
and levels when scaling enabled. Disabled diagnostics including missing are
preserved. missing_config never yields success. Every numeric output is checked
for finiteness. No partial successful result after any failure.

## 9. EN27 disposition

Resolution shape: kind:resolution, encounterId, definitionId, ruleId, outcome.
Cancellation shape: kind:cancellation, encounterId, definitionId, reasonId,
outcome:null. Outcome is side-based victory or draw. IDs preserve bytes; unknown,
overlapping or cross-bound IDs fail TypeError. Close keeps the same semantic record.
ruleId records the selected rule, but structural records do not authenticate
historical execution provenance; L4 must retain the accepted record.

## 10. Test-only atomic acceptance

baseline, and accepts state+record together. Deliberate atomic failure leaves both
unchanged. Competing candidates accept at most once. Forged structural snapshots

production transaction service or global gameplay store.

## 11. EN28 explicit close

## 12. Close rejection

record is TypeError before domain no-op. Resolved+cancellation record is invalid.

active (empty allowed). Request encounterId/reasonId. Only allowed prepared/active
states propose cancelled; denied policy→cancellation_not_allowed;

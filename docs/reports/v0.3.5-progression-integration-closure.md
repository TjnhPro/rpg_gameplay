or deferred confirmed defects. Reproduction/expected-vs-actual/fix: not applicable;
all new evidence matched the independently specified expected behavior. This is

Caller owns EXP curves, eligibility, costs, reward maps and consistent cumulative/
local mode use. All level/reward/allocation/recalculation/upgrade operations are
explicit. No Skill execution/cooldown/effect, persistence, networking, Text/2D/2.5D
game or global transaction/locking exists. Caller must thread current snapshots
and apply combined results together. Updated cursor/rank guards reject replay;
old immutable inputs may branch. Structural receipts validate shape/consistency,
not trusted provenance/authentication. There is no persistent exactly-once
guarantee or concurrent-write resolver. These accepted limitations are not hidden
by a second engine/store and do not constitute new closure defects.

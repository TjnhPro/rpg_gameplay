## Implementation / public surface

entities, actorLife, policy). SK07 independently checks definition-instance and
Life-actor consistency (TypeError) and actor binding/explicit owned (rejection).
SK08 consumes required/non-required rank policy: null means unregistered, zero
is registered/unlearned, current numeric ranks nonnegative safe integers, minimum
positive safe integer. SK09 uses public getEntity and canonical EntityLifeState,
no HP inference, kind policy or transition. SK10 validates the whole invocation
before selecting actor_not_found > actor_dead > skill_not_owned >
skill_rank_unregistered > skill_rank_too_low > actor_ineligible > none.

SkillAdmissionStatus, SkillAdmissionReason, SkillAdmissionResult. Result is exactly
{instanceId, skillId, actorEntityId, status, reason}, newly detached/frozen scalar
data. All identity strings remain exact. Records require own data fields with
no unknown keys/symbols/hidden extras/accessors/inherited required fields; getters
are not invoked. Life follows Entity's ordinary/null prototype contract.

compiler symbols (prior67/98 +1/5). No helper leak, any, deep export or old schema
change. Package remains private, no runtime dependency added. Version-only lock
